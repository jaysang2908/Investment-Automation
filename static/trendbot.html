Intraday Momentum Bot

Long-only gap-continuation system on the S&P 500. 5-minute cycle: gap ≥3% on ≥2× relative volume, entries on strength above premarket high and HOD, 1% account risk per trade, partial at +0.75R, breakeven at +1R, swing-low trail, flat by 15:51 ET. Executed on an IBKR paper account — results are simulated fills, not real money.

Screening Funnel — why the bot said no

Each bar is how many of the day's evaluations that rule killed. One evaluation can fail several rules at once, so bars sum to more than 100%. A rule sitting near 100% every day is the binding constraint — that is the dial to turn if the bot is trading too rarely.
