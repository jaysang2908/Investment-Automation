Credit Ratings

Senior unsecured ratings from S&P and Moody's for all covered tickers. The more conservative rating auto-feeds into each ticker's WACC cost-of-debt assumption.

S&P · Moody's Loading…
Methodology: When S&P and Moody's diverge, the lower (more conservative) rating is used as the WACC Input — the pre-tax cost of debt spread applied to each ticker's WACC calculation. Moody's ratings are converted to S&P-equivalent scale before comparison (Aaa→AAA, Aa1→AA+, A1→A+, Baa1→BBB+, Ba1→BB+, etc.). Tickers flagged Split-Rated have a divergence of ≥ 2 notches or cross the investment-grade / high-yield boundary. Spreads follow the Damodaran Jan 2025 US default spread table. Source: S&P Global Ratings and Moody's Investors Service public announcements. Refreshed quarterly.
